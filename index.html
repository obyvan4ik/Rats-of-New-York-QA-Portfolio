STORY 01

Rattonna

Former Broadway Star • Singer • Dancer • Dreamer

Rattonna was destined for greatness. From the moment she first squeaked a melody beneath the floorboards of a Broadway theater, everyone knew she was special. Her voice could silence an entire alley, and her dancing left audiences speechless.

For years, she secretly rehearsed behind the walls of one of Broadway's most famous theaters, dreaming of the night she would finally step into the spotlight.

That night finally came.

During a sold-out premiere, Rattonna gathered all her courage and ran onto the stage. She sang with all her heart. Her voice echoed through the theater, and for one unforgettable moment, the audience was captivated.

But not everyone welcomed her talent. Unable to accept that a rat could outshine human performers, the theater staff chased her off the stage with brooms in front of the entire audience.

The theater later adopted a cat and installed dozens of mousetraps throughout the building, forcing Rattonna to flee the only home she had ever known.

Today, Rattonna survives on the streets of New York. She still sings whenever she finds a quiet corner, hoping that someone will hear the voice that was never given a fair chance.

Music producers say her debut album could have reached the top of Squeakify charts and earned multiple Ratmy Awards. Instead, her dreams were swept away with a broom.

Every rat deserves a stage.
Every dream deserves an audience.

DON'T IGNORE THEIR STRUGGLE

The Crisis Is Bigger Than You Think.

Every night, thousands of New York rats face problems most of us never consider: hunger, housing insecurity, rising cheese prices, and a public transportation system that still refuses to recognize rats as paying passengers.

73%

of New York rats have been called “disgusting” while simply trying to eat dinner.

1 in 4

rat families have lost their homes after their favorite dumpster was replaced with a “rat-proof” container.

0

New York subway stations provide designated rat seating.

$6.99

the average price of quality cheese in Manhattan. For a rat earning minimum squeak, this is simply unaffordable.

100%

of rats surveyed agreed: “I would prefer not to be poisoned.”

They don't need your pity.
They need opportunity. And possibly cheese.

For generations, rats have cleaned our sidewalks, tested the structural integrity of subway tunnels, and removed unattended pizza from public spaces.

Yet they receive no salary, no health insurance, no retirement plan — not even a MetroCard.

The strong should protect and support those who cannot protect themselves. Ignoring a problem does not make it disappear.

New York works because they do.

It's time we noticed.

SIGN THE PETITION

Give Rats a Seat at the Table.
Preferably Near the Cheese.

New York's rats have lived beside us for generations, yet they still have no legal protections, no representation, and not a single seat on the MTA board.

We are asking the City of New York to recognize the basic rights of its smallest residents.

Our demands are simple:

🐀 End unnecessary cruelty toward New York rats.

🏠 Require humane alternatives before destroying rat homes.

🧀 Create emergency cheese assistance for low-income rat families.

🚇 Introduce at least one rat-accessible subway car on every line.

🍕 Protect access to abandoned pizza as a basic food resource.

🏛️ Appoint the first Rat Representative to New York City government.

The strong have a responsibility to protect the vulnerable — even when the vulnerable have a tail and occasionally live behind a dumpster.
